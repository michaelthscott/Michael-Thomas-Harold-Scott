- Thomas Francis Ord Rippingham
- Harold Taggart Scott

The good thing about middle names is that 
they confer a degree of uniqueness. 

I didn't know it at the time, when I first
learned to identify with M. T. H. S. on
my Inchmarlo socks, but this uniqueness 
would come in handy when I entered the 
world of namespaces. 

My older brother Christopher was given 
our paternal great grandfather's name 
William. 

When I came along the presiding authority 
in these matters, probably my father's mother,
decided that both grandfathers had to be 
honoured equally, thus I got two middle 
names Thomas Harold. I wonder how they 
decided the order. 

Later my younger brother Timothy would inherit
our father's name Noel as his middle name. 

All boys names because we were boys, but 
my mother did confide to me once that it
had already been decided that if i was a
girl I would be Janet.
